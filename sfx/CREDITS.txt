Shelf sound effects (public/sfx/atlas.bin, atlas.json).

Built by tools/sfx/build-atlas.py from recordings released under CC0 (public domain dedication), processed
(cleaned, warmed, loudness matched, trimmed, 16 kHz mono) and layered. No attribution is required; credit is given anyway:
- Kenney (kenney.nl): Impact Sounds, RPG Audio, Interface Sounds. CC0.
- OpenGameArt.org: "Scrapes" (CC0), "Light Switch Turn On And Off SFX" by yd (CC0),
  "20 Rustles of dry leaves" (CC0), "Sound Effects Pack" by Owlish Media (CC0).
The zoom and view-switch whooshes are synthesised by the same script.
